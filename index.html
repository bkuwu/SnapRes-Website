Inside The App

True stretch, one tap away

Every resolution you play on, right on the main screen, each with a badge that tells you if Windows is ready for it.

Add to Windows, in one click

“Not available” just means Windows doesn't know that resolution yet. Hover it, click “Add to Windows” and approve the prompt. It works with NVIDIA, AMD and Intel, and never adds anything your monitor can't display. Right-click an available one to remove it.

i

Adding a resolution

Windows is about to add this resolution. Your screen may go black for a moment and then turn back on. This is safe, so don't worry. It's just how the resolution gets added to Windows.

The monitor driver, one switch away

Turn it off from the card above the resolutions, approve one Windows prompt, and your stretch finally looks stretched. Turn it back on and you return to your native resolution.

A setup guide that does the work

“Go” takes you to the right spot and “Fix” switches your GPU scaling to full screen for you. Every step has clear notes for NVIDIA, AMD and Intel.

Revert back just as fast

Match's over? Snap straight back to your native resolution with the same single click.

Or type your own

Play something outside the presets? Type any width × height, apply it, and save it as a profile. Saved profiles get the same badge and the same Add to Windows button.
