Privacy Policy

Who this covers

This policy covers the SnapRes website at snapres.online, run by bku. You can reach me at hakobennadja66@gmail.com.

What the site collects

The site counts visits and how many people are on it right now, using a small counter service. It also uses GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics tool, to see which pages are viewed and when the download button is clicked. Your browser also stores your theme, language and whether your download was already counted, in local storage on your own device.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage or switch off personalized ads in Google Ads Settings or at aboutads.info. You can read how Google uses data from sites that use its services here. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Downloads

The installer is hosted on GitHub, so GitHub's own privacy policy applies when you download it.

Your choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings, use the ad settings above, or contact me with any question or request about your data.
